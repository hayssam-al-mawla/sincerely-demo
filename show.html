Latest conversation, with Leah Thomas

How We Treat the Earth Mirrors How We Treat Each Other

Leah Thomas lines the ceiling with vintage parachutes so that everyone underneath them can picture the world they are building together. She is an environmental author, advocate, and content creator who works at the intersection of climate and culture. She hosts Earth Sessions climate concerts, is finishing a book on ecofeminism called Earth as Mother, and runs a local crafting club where people learn to mend and sew, because joy and sustainable skill-building belong together.
