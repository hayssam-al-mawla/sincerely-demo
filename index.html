Katherine Dudtschak

Founder and CEO of Sincerely, author of the memoir Sincerely, Katherine. and a former bank CEO with nearly four decades in leadership.

She grew up on an Ontario farm, the daughter of German immigrants who had lived through war and displacement. In 2019, while a senior executive at RBC, she affirmed her gender to her colleagues. The compassion she received, and the stories people then entrusted to her, became the foundation of Sincerely.

The deeper work

Sincerely Human

Beneath the stories lives a body of research and original thinking about how we solve problems as human beings: from fear and ego, or from kindness and curiosity.

It asks how the systems we build mirror our inner state, and what is uniquely and innately human in an age of AI. The human architecture, the journey from performance to presence, and the Three Pillars of Transformation all live here.

The black sheep.

This week’s letter, November 10, 2026

A few days ago, I was talking with a friend, someone I care about and deeply admire. We were talking about family, about the things we carry through our lives, and about her journey of breaking a cycle of intergenerational trauma.

At one point, she referred to herself as the black sheep of the family.

Those words stopped me. I have never seen her as a black sheep. I see an extraordinary mother, an incredibly curious, creative and talented human being, and someone who is beautiful inside and out. Someone who has found the courage to journey deeply within herself, to embrace more of who she truly is, and to live from that place. Her essence. Her uniqueness. Her beauty.
