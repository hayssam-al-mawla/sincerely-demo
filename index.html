Katherine Dudtschak

Founder and CEO of Sincerely, author of the memoir Sincerely, Katherine. and a former bank CEO with nearly four decades in leadership.

She grew up on an Ontario farm, the daughter of German immigrants who had lived through war and displacement. In 2019, while a senior executive at RBC, she affirmed her gender to her colleagues. The compassion she received, and the stories people then entrusted to her, became the foundation of Sincerely.

The memoir

Sincerely, Katherine.

One woman's honest account of finding her way home to herself while leading at the highest levels of corporate life.

A story of secrets kept and finally told, and of what leadership looks like from wholeness instead of fear. Offered in case it helps you find your own way home.

“Her story might not be yours, but don't be fooled, its lessons are universally ours.” Sophie Grégoire Trudeau, bestselling author of Closer Together

Work with Sincerely

Transformational leadership for the world of today and tomorrow. Three complementary ways of working, each adapted to the person, group or institution.

Sincerely Dialogue

Create the conditions.

Human stories, kind curiosity, deep listening and shared inquiry for leadership teams and boards.

Speaking, lecturing and learning

Develop perspective and practice.

Keynotes, lectures and a five-module learning series on the essence and practice of transformational leadership.

Sincerely Advisory

Steward and sustain.

Selective, confidential counsel for boards, CEOs and executive teams, and for private clients at consequential moments.
