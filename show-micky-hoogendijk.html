The Sincerely Show, with Micky Hoogendijk

Vulnerability Is the Strength, and the Art of Creating Space to Move Forward

She places strength and vulnerability side by side and calls them one thing.

October 15, 2026, 1 hr 22 min

  • Creativity
  • Healing
Listen on Spotify Apple Podcasts Watch on YouTube

Micky Hoogendijk is a self-taught artist working across photography, sculpture, painting, and stone carving, and the author of a memoir on the art of letting go. She creates bronze figures of people holding onto one another, coaxes stillness from her photographic subjects until they stop performing and simply arrive, and lives from what she calls her child energy. A photograph of seven-year-old Miriam lives on her phone. That child, she says, is the one she works for.

In this conversation, Micky and Katherine walk backward through a life lived across twenty-four moves, many countries, and as many forms of expression. They talk about The Artist’s Way and the morning pages that opened Micky’s creative door; about Eric Morris, the acting teacher who asked her to say “I’m a little girl, will you please be my friend?” in front of twenty-five working actors, and what she understood in the tears that followed. They talk about the army of bronze figures she began during the first week of COVID, “the ones,” people holding onto each other. And they talk about her memoir and what she discovered writing it: that letting go means creating space to keep moving forward.

This conversation is for anyone who was once laughed at for being wholly themselves, and who is finding their way back to that child.

“I actually think and believe that vulnerability is the strength and it's the most beautiful part of a human being.”Micky Hoogendijk
