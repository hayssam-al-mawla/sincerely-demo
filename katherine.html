Katherine Dudtschak

Founder and CEO of Sincerely. Author of Sincerely, Katherine. Host of The Sincerely Show. A former bank CEO whose own transformation became the seed of an institute.

Invite Katherine to speak Read the memoir

Before you hear my story, let me tell you who I am underneath it.

I am curious. Endlessly curious. As a child, I took apart our radio looking for the man inside it.

I am playful. I believe joy is not a reward for work well done but the very ground we're meant to walk on.

I am someone who sees patterns. My mind moves differently. It took me years to understand this as a gift.

And I am still learning. Still growing. Still becoming.

From a farm in Ontario to a farm by the lake

One profound personal transformation and three major leadership transformations. Distinct experiences, deeply connected.

  1. The farmChildhood, rural Ontario

    Eight acres and a secret

    Katherine grew up on a small farm, the daughter of German immigrants whose lives had been shaped by war and displacement. From as far back as she can remember she knew who she was on the inside, and she kept that secret for nearly fifty years.

  2. 13Her first job

    A mind that works in patterns

    Her report cards said she should focus more. Nobody knew she was dyslexic. She started working at thirteen, put herself through school, married, had four children in four years, and built a banking career that would span nearly four decades.

  3. 2013CEO, RBC Caribbean

    Saving a bank and its livelihoods

    She led operations across 19 countries through a consequential turnaround. Candour, compassion and trust anchored the renewal, and sustainable profitability returned in about 24 months.

  4. 2015Executive Vice President, Regional Banking, RBC

    Twenty-five thousand colleagues

    Responsible for roughly a third of RBC's workforce and more than 15 million clients, she transformed the nature of the work with deep attention to inclusion, care and advisor capability.

  5. 2019Coming home to herself

    She affirmed her gender to 80,000 colleagues

    She recorded a video for the whole bank and expected rejection. What began in fear unfolded into love, acceptance and standing ovations. Colleagues began entrusting her with their own stories, which deepened her understanding of the burdens people carry and the potential they hold.

  6. 2022Catalyst Honours

    Business Leader Champion

    Recognized for inclusive leadership that enabled exceptional, sustained business performance. She went on to lead HomeEquity Bank as CEO, where the asset base grew past C$10 billion.

  7. TodayMaison Millefleurs, Prince Edward County

    Sincerely

    She left banking, found a farm, planted lavender and welcomed honeybees. From there she founded Sincerely, hosts The Sincerely Show, writes Sincerely Letters, and works with a small number of leaders and institutions.

    Work with Katherine

Executive judgment and deep human understanding

Her life and leadership are not separate sources of credibility. Together they form the foundation of Sincerely.

Katherine serves on the board of the Canadian Museum for Human Rights. She is a citizen of Canada and the European Union, and a mother of four.
